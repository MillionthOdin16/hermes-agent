## 2026-10-11 - ChatSessionList ARIA ListItems
**Learning:** Screen readers cannot infer the context of list items that just render the chat session title directly. Applying an `aria-label` directly on the `ListItem` or `button` can improve the accessibility of navigation UI. However, adding `aria-label` to container elements that hold complex content (like preview text, timestamps, or badges) can obscure that content if the label is too simple, as screen readers will only announce the label.
**Action:** When adding accessibility labels to list items, prefer using `aria-labelledby` or ensuring the label includes all essential contextual information, rather than overriding everything with just the title. For simple elements like a generic "New Chat" button, a simple `aria-label` is sufficient and helpful. Always ensure localization variables exist (e.g. `t.sessions.newChat` and `t.sessions.selectSession`) before applying them.
