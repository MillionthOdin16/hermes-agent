## 2026-06-25 - Hoisting Regexes in Signal Formatting Adapter
**Learning:** In the gateway platform adapters (specifically `gateway/platforms/signal_format.py`), `markdown_to_signal` was compiling 8 separate regular expressions and generating two inner functions (`_utf16_len`, `_normalize_bullet_markers`) on *every single message* being formatted for Signal. Python's `re.compile()` has a small internal cache, but re-evaluating the `def` and list instantiations still creates overhead.
**Action:** Always verify if high-traffic utility functions (like message formatters or parsers) are compiling regexes or defining closures inline. Extracting them to the module-level eliminates this repeated work, especially in daemons like the `gateway` that process many messages per second.
